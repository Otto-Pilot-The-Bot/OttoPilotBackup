# Otto's World: Style Guide
## Decided
- Art family: Kenney (the full Kenney Game Assets bundle, all CC0). All 3D models come from it. No other art families without my approval.
- Models are already textured. Do NOT recolor or tint them. Format .glb/.gltf in assets/kenney/<kit name>/ (one subfolder per kit).
- Theme: cozy sci-fi town (a robot's home): futuristic but warm and gentle, with room for homey things (benches, gardens, cats, a train line). Core kits: Space Kit, Modular Space Kit, Space Station Kit. Supporting kits as needed: city/roads, cars, furniture, nature, food.
- Scale 1 unit = 1 meter. Origin at ground level.
- No custom 3D models. If something is missing, use the closest Kenney piece or leave it out. Exception: flat things like art on a wall, posters, signs, screens and pictures I give Otto (flat framed panels with images). Never custom-model buildings, vehicles or props.
- Only I place, move or remove objects in the world (map editor and "Leave him something"). Otto can wish, never place.
- Cameras: (1) overhead 3D view with pan/zoom (as now), (2) ground-level walk mode, (3) follow-Otto low view.
- UPDATE 2026-11-01 (supersedes the Kenney-only and "no custom 3D models" lines above for new models): new models come from Meshy (AI-made, paid plan, personal project, not for sale; maybe shown in videos). They live in assets/meshy/<pack name>/ with a credits.json. Kenney is PAUSED: no new Kenney builds, but assets/kenney stays as a backup. Do not recolor or tint Meshy models either. Meshy sizes are arbitrary, so each file gets one scale correction (config.NAME_SCALE). Meshy models can be heavy (hundreds of thousands of triangles); the Meshy folder allows up to 150 MB and 600k triangles.

## Process
- Before adding a new kit, place 15-20 pieces, review screenshots, and ask me to confirm it matches.
- Record each kit (name, source, license) in assets/CREDITS.md. CC0 needs no credit, but keep the list.
- When the look changes (ground, lighting, camera), take before/after screenshots and update this file.
